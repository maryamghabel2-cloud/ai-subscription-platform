**Date:** 2026-07-19

## Referral (User-to-User)

### Idea 1: Share Result with Watermark + Referral Link
- After image generation, user can share result with small watermark "Made with Persian AI Workspace + referral link"
- New user clicks link, signs up, both get small credits (e.g., 5 credits)
- **Risk:** Credit issuance above threshold requires human approval (see HUMAN_APPROVAL_GATES). For MVP, referral credit issuance needs approval config
- **Approval:** Issuing credits above threshold requires approval

### Idea 2: Invite Friend - Credit for Both
- Dashboard has invite link, shows referrals
- Future, needs wallet logic

## Affiliate (Creator/Business)

### Idea 1: Creator Affiliate
- Creator (Instagram seller) shares affiliate link to product studio
- If their follower buys credit pack, creator gets 20% credit commission
- Needs affiliate dashboard, tracking

### Idea 2: Developer Affiliate
- Developer refers other developers to API platform

## Safety & Approval

- Credit issuance above threshold → approval required
- No auto bulk credit issuance
- No spam referral (rate limit invite send, no bulk email without approval)
- Referral links must have anti-fraud (unique per user, no self-referral)

## Metrics

- Referral visits, referral signup conversion, referral activation, fraud rate
