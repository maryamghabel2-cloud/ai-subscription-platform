# ADR 0001: Canonical Commercial Roadmap

## Status
Owner Approved — 2026-08-11.

## Decision
The product pivot is Persian AI Business Automation + Creator Commerce Platform. The canonical phase model is Phase 0 Completed, Phase 1 In Progress, Phase 2-4 Planned, and Phase 5 Future.

## Rationale
The prior roadmap predates accepted commercial direction and conflicts with current foundation evidence.

## Consequences
Backend and frontend implementation work follows Phase 1. Backlog work is ordered against the canonical roadmap. Legacy and recovery documents are historical rather than current requirements.
