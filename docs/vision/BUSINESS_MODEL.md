# Business Model

**Status:** Owner Approved commercial direction — 2026-08-11

The platform is a Persian AI Business Automation and Creator Commerce Platform,
not a SaaS subscription-resale or shared-credential service.

## Commercial Model

The first product is Persian Content and Commerce Studio. The second is Persian
Business Agent Pack. Revenue directions include credits, product subscriptions,
creator marketplace commission, agency/white-label offerings, and later enterprise
setup. Exact pricing, commission, payout, and payment mechanisms remain open
business and legal decisions.

## Billing Foundation

The repository contains wallet, ledger, and mock payment-intent foundation code.
Credit denomination, real gateway activation, and commercial settlement are not
established by this document. Usage reserve/settle work remains Phase 1 scope.

## Value Exchange

Users receive Persian-first chat, Skills, studios, creator services, and eventually
bounded business automation. Sellers and creators provide licensed, reviewed work.
No shared account, credential resale, or provider-account resale is permitted.
