# Product Vision

**Status:** Owner Approved commercial direction — 2026-08-11

The product is a Persian-first Multimodal AI Platform for creators and businesses.
It combines a workspace activation layer, commercial studios, marketplaces, and
later bounded Business Agent automation. It is not a shared-account or credential
resale product.

## Product Lines

### First Commercial Product: Persian Content and Commerce Studio

- Reels & Shorts Auto Editor
- Instagram Caption & Hashtag Generator
- Product Photography Studio
- Product-to-Video Ad Generator
- UGC Creator Marketplace
- Persian SEO Generator

Outputs are reviewable; external publishing requires its own approval boundary.

### Second Commercial Product: Persian Business Agent Pack

The future pack includes bounded Support, Sales, E-commerce, Website/SEO, and
Document/Administration agents under policy, sandbox, audit, and approval controls.

## Activation Capabilities

General Persian Chat is the activation and onboarding capability. Prompt Enhancer
is the first paid Skill. Credit-based billing uses the existing wallet and ledger
foundation; this document does not activate a real payment provider.

## Roadmap Alignment

Phase 1 is the Core Product MVP in progress. Commercial Studio, Marketplace, Agent
Platform, and Scale phases follow the canonical [Master Roadmap](../roadmap/MASTER_ROADMAP.md).
Planned scope must not be read as implementation evidence.
