# BLOG REQUIREMENTS

**Date:** 2026-07-19

## Purpose
SEO and education: Persian AI guides, prompt tips, product photography tips, etc.

## Structure

- /blog - List with categories (Prompt, Product Photo, Telegram, Personas, API, Business)
- /blog/{slug} - Article

## Article Template

- H1
- Intro with search intent answer in first 100 words
- Table of contents (H2 anchor links)
- H2 sections with how-to, examples
- Internal links to tool landing, persona, product studio
- FAQ Section (Schema FAQPage)
- CTA: Try tool
- Author: Team, date, disclaimer if persona-related
- Tags

## Content Workflow

- SEO Content Agent drafts brief → outline → draft
- Research checks sources
- Compliance checks risky claims
- Growth Marketing reviews CTA
- Human approval → publish manually

## SEO

- Meta title/description, alt text, internal links, schema: BlogPosting + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList
- Sitemap includes blog posts
- No auto-publishing without review

## Safety

- No guarantee claims
- Persona-related blog includes disclaimer
- No medical/legal/psych advice without disclaimer + compliance review
